EMS daily sync

Every day at 11:00 UTC the dashboard takes the DGG member roster and the participating companies of every current or forthcoming event whose registration has opened from EMS. Concluded events are never changed. Nothing is written when EMS does not answer or returns far fewer companies than we hold, so the last upload stays in place: the uploads below are the fallback. EMS does not yet supply currency, 52-week range, 1-year return, production, reserves, resources or the webcast page link; those are kept from the last upload.

Upload Companies (fallback for the EMS sync)

Use when EMS cannot supply the roster. Delete and replace: the companies table and all event participations are cleared and the companies reloaded from the CSV, so re-upload each event's participations afterwards (and reload the hand-loaded MFA25 rows). The next EMS sync then refreshes the roster and any event whose registration is open.

Briefings (PDF)

Generate a fresh three-page, Denver Gold Group and event-branded briefing from the data loaded at this moment, so upload the latest attendees, meetings and holdings first. The Analyst Briefing is for sell-side analysts: issuers and market cap, shareholder representation, the audience and its C-suite presence, 1x1 meetings and the metals backdrop, then after the forum the meetings by stage and metal, webcast performance and the forums to come. The Member and Partner Post-Show Briefing is for presenting issuers and event partners after the forum: the same figures, led by who was in the room, what they own and the meetings held, plus the event partners, webcast performance and the forums to come. Partners come from the PSR sponsors table for the event.
Webcast performance (Member briefing, page 3). Paste the webcast platform's per-status table (Status, Total Views, Average Views, Total Duration, Average Duration) for this year and for last year's full year. Only indices are printed, never the counts.
MiningForum.live views (the news and analysis outlet, not the presentation site), one month per line as YYYY-MM: views. Printed as a rate per 30 days (the current month counts its days to date), so give last year the forum month and the month after.
Fill the two investor fields from the Total Confirmed Meeting Counts export:
The meeting total itself comes from the Member Meetings upload (Meetings Dashboard export). Only the three totals are kept from the investor file, never names or emails. These figures are saved with the event when you generate. Before the event starts, meetings and investor requests are shown as projections of the final tally using the multiplier, which is never printed; afterwards the actual totals are used.

Shareholder representation for the Analyst Briefing. Upload the Member Holdings export (.xlsx or .csv), or paste the table, with columns MC Range, Members, Total MC, Average MC, Attendee Holdings.
